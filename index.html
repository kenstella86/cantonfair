While You're Here — Guangzhou Insider Guide

A quick guide for your first 72 hours in Guangzhou during the Canton Fair.

Must-Eat

Dim Sum

Look for places with long local queues before 10am.

Roast Goose

Ask your hotel concierge for the nearest good one.

Wonton Noodles

Find noodle shops with woks in the window.

Claypot Rice

Try one at a local night market after the fair.

Must-See

Canton Tower

Sunset bar on the 107th floor.

Shamian Island

Colonial walkways · free · 40 min from Pazhou.

Pearl River Cruise

Night cruise at 8pm · around ¥100.

Chen Clan Academy

Metro Line 1 · beautiful ancestral hall.

Getting Around

  • To the fair: Metro Line 8 → Pazhou Complex Station.
  • Taxis: Use the DiDi app (English interface available).
  • Payment: Cards at hotels/malls. Carry cash for street food.
